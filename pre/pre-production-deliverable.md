# 1. Narrative

Title: The Blooming

Genre: 2D Point-and-Click Psychological Horror

## Introduction

Blooming is a 2D point-and-click psychological horror game developed around a flora-themed narrative. The game follows a young botanist who is assigned to care for a rare and mysterious plant. As he continues to nurture the plant, strange events begin to occur. The plant gradually invades his mind through whispers and hallucinations, eventually manipulating him, becoming its final source of nourishment.

The game focuses on environmental storytelling, puzzle-solving, inventory interaction, and event-driven horror sequences. Visual and audio special effects will be used extensively to create an immersive horror experience while fulfilling the flora-themed requirements of the assignment.

## Character

### Elias

**Role:** Main Character, Junior Botanist

**Abilities:**

* **Observation**

Allows the player to inspect objects and gather clues.

* **Journal Analysis**

Records important information, notes, and puzzle hints.

* **Sanity Awareness**

Displays the protagonist's mental condition. Lower sanity levels trigger hallucinations and visual distortions.

### Mystery flower

**Role:** Main Enemy, the plant that is taken care of by the main character

**Abilities:**

* Causes insanity to the main character
* Wants to eat anything for nutrition
* Makes the main character see hallucinations

## Story Overview

### Story Background

The main character, Elias, is a junior botanist working at an isolated botanical research facility. He is assigned to care for a newly discovered flower seed from an uncharted forest.

Initially, it seems harmless, but as time goes by, its growth patterns start changing and speeding up, with its roots growing out of the flowerpot. As nighttime arrived, Elais started hearing faint whispers wherever he approached the plant. The whispers slowly grow louder and more coherent. He begins seeing shadows moving between flowerpots, roots crawling across walls, and strange figures hidden among the greenhouse plants.

As the plant grows larger, Elias becomes increasingly obsessed with protecting it. Research documents reveal that previous caretakers disappeared under mysterious circumstances. Despite discovering these warnings, Elias finds himself unable to resist the plant's influence.

Eventually, the plant reveals its true nature. It survives by consuming living organisms and manipulating victims through hypnosis. Under its control, Elias willingly offers himself as nourishment and is swallowed whole by the giant flower.

The game ends with a new caretaker arriving at the facility, while a flower bearing Elias's face quietly blooms among the greenhouse plants.
