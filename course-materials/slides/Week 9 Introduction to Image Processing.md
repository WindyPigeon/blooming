## Comparisons

| Digital Image Processing | Computer Graphic |
| --- | --- |
| Use algorithms to perform operations to enhance, analyze, or manipulate image. | Creating, manipulating, and rendering visual content using computers. |
| Focus on improving the quality and extracting information from existing images. | Focus on generating new images and animations from scratch. |
| Technique: image enhancement, restoration, compression, segmentation, and feature extraction. | Technique: modeling, animation, rendering, and shading. |
| Output: enhanced or analyzed versions of existing images. | Output: New images or animations from models and scenes |
