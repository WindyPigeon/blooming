# Aims of this Module

This module introduces students to the art and science of special/visual effects composition using digital image processing and computer graphic approaches. Students will learn the technical concepts behind synthetic effects creation. They will exercise their computer graphic skills to express their creative thinking to generate various synthetic imagery effects.
