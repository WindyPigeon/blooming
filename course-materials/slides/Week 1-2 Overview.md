## Introduction to Special Effects

- Special effects referred to as practical effects

- Involve physical and mechanical techniques used in filmmaking to create visual illusions that cannot be achieved through standard filming alone.

- Created on the set during production and include techniques

  - Makeup, animatronics,

  - pyrotechnics, and

  - practical props.

## Introduction to Visual Effects

- Visual effects known as digital effects

- Computer-generated or digitally manipulated elements added in post-production to enhance or create visual elements in a film or other media.

  - CGI (Computer-Generated Imagery),

  - Green screen compositing,

  - Digital animation.

## Differences

- **Special Effects**

  - Created in the physical world, often on the set during filming.

  - Involve skilled artisans and craftsmanship.

  - Examples - makeup to transform actors, animatronic puppets to create lifelike creatures, explosions, and miniatures.

- **Visual Effects**

  - Created or manipulated in post-production using computer software and technology.

  - Involve visual effects artists and technicians.

  - Examples - CGI to generate fictional creatures, altering the background with green screen compositing, and adding 3D elements to scenes.

## The Roles

- **Films**

  - Transform ordinary scenes into extraordinary ones.

  - Practical effects, such as makeup and animatronics (creatures and characters)

  - Digital effects - to realize complex, otherworldly environments and creatures.

- **Games**

  - Enhance gameplay and narrative.

  - Responsive Environment - dynamic lighting and particle systems

- **Image**

  - Enhance photographs - create stunning and imaginative digital art.

  - Create a desired atmosphere or style on static images

- **Animation**

  - Bring characters/ environment to life.

    - Characters: facial expressions, body movement.

    - Environment: weather effects, explosions, magical spells,  fire.

- **Sound**

  - Enhance the emotional impact of scenes - unique musical textures and styles, realistic or surreal soundscapes.

## Computer-generated imagery (CGI)

- Refers to the application of computer graphics to create or manipulate visual elements.

- **Can be used to**

  - Create 3D models of objects, characters, or environments in a virtual space using specialized software.

  - Apply textures to 3D models to give them realistic appearances, including details like colors, patterns, and surface properties.

  - Create dynamic, animated sequences (special effects)

  - Simulate physical phenomena like fire, smoke, water, and physics-based interactions.

## Computer-generated imagery (CGI)

- Combine multiple visual elements, including live-action footage, to create a seamless and integrated final image or scene.

- Converts 3D models and scenes into 2D images or animation.

## Visual Effects Components

- **Model**

  - 3D /2D Modelling

  - Models the characters, objects, environments, and special effects elements.

- **Animation**

  - Defining movements, actions, and behaviour.

  - E.g. Characters, creatures and objects animation

- **Compositing**

  - Scene composition by combining different elements (environment, models, and visual effects)

- **Colour**

  - Adjust and enhance the colours and tones of a object / scene.

- **Lighting**

  - Object depth and realism to the visuals.

- **Shading**

  - Object positioning (lighting and shadow).

- **Texturing**

  - Object / Surface details, textures, and materials

# Visual Effects Components

- Sound Design

  - Sound effects, music, and audio elements to enhance the overall sensory experience.

- Particle Systems

  - Simulate natural phenomena - smoke, fire, rain, snow, and explosions.

- Physics Simulation

  - Create object behaviour (replicate real event)

# Visual Effects Workflow

**Guideline to follow**

- Pre-production

  - **Concept and Planning**

    -  Define VFX requirements

    - Identify scenarios and moments that will require VFX

  - **Storyboarding and Previsualization**

    - Create storyboards or previsualizations on VFX elements

2. Production

   - **Data Acquisition**

     - Collect necessary data, such as reference images, textures, and 3D models, to assist in VFX integration.

     - Capture reference points and markers (motion)

3. Post Production

   - **Editing and Post-Production Planning**

     - Set the technical and creative guidelines for integrating VFX

     - Develop a detailed plan for how VFX elements will be used in the scenario

   - **Modelling and Animation**

     - Create models of characters, objects, and environmental elements

     - Animate the models (character/object movements)

   - **Texture and Shading**

     - Apply textures and shaders to the models to achieve realistic appearances.

   - **Lighting and Rendering**

     -Set up scene lighting to match the visual style and mood to fix different scenes and scenario

   - **Particle Systems and Physics Simulation**

     - Create and fine-tune particle systems and physics simulations to generate real-time VFX

   - **Compositing**

     - Implement compositing techniques to combine model, and VFX effects to align with scenario and moments.

   - **Visual Effects (VFX)**

     - Implement events to trigger the visual effects.

   - **Sound Design**

     - Integrate sound effects, music, and audio elements

   - **Quality Control and Review**

     - Review the VFX to identify and correct any issues or inconsistencies and align with the game's creative vision and storytelling goals

4. Finalization

   - **Final Editing**

     - Make any final adjustments to the overall VFX integration within the game, considering gameplay balance and narrative coherence.

   - **Rendering and Delivery:**

     - Ensure render VFX assets in artefact seamlessly - incorporate all visual and audio effects.

5. Quality Assurance and Feedback

   - **Gather Feedback**

     - Ensure VFX meet the desired standards and requirements

6. **Documentation and Archiving**

   - Documentation

     - Document the processes, assets, and technical details for future maintenance - updates, expansions, or adaptations.
