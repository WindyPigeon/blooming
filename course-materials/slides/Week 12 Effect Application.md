## Rendering Techniques

- Real-time

  - Controlling a character's during a game, with the script executing every frame.

- Offline

  - Pre-rendering sequence of movements from a script and then compositing it into a final video or film. 

## Computer-generated Imagery (CGI)

- Create still or animated visual content using computer software.

- Using computer graphics to create or manipulate visual content.

- Enhances or replaces real-world visuals to create believable, immersive scenes

- Widely associated with the film industry

- Application

  - Film Industry : Realistic creatures, Environments, Special effects

  - Game: Characters, Environments.

  - Commercial : Marketing & Advertising

## CG Animation Techniques

- Keyframe

  - Define frames and interpolates between them.

- Procedural

  - Write algorithms to generate motion , e.g. A* for path finding

- Scripting

  - Write code to control animation behavior dynamically. E.g. projectile motion principles

- Motion

  - Captures real-world movement and maps it to digital characters.

  - Apply mathematical formulas or rules to generate motion.

## Sound

- Using of audio elements to enhance, complement, and convey information in various forms of media,

  - Film / video games / online content.

- Sets the atmosphere, mood, evokes emotions, emphasizes actions, and creates a sense of immersion.

- Purpose : Enhance audience's experience

- Artificially created or enhanced sounds used to emphasize actions, create environments, or add realism.

## Role of Sound

- Enhancing Realism.

- Emphasizing Action

- Creating Mood and Atmosphere

- Supporting Storytelling

- Example

  - Rain, city traffic, or birds chirping.

  - clothes rustling, doors opening, and glass breaking.

  - footsteps, explosions, and ambient noises.
